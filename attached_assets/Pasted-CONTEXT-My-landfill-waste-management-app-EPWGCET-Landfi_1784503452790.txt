CONTEXT
My landfill/waste-management app (EPWGCET Landfill Tracker) has two 
payment-tracking systems that are NOT connected to each other:

1. OLD SYSTEM: `invoices` table (fields: totalAmount, paidAmount, status, 
   paidAt) — updated by `generateInvoice()` and `updateInvoice()` in 
   PageInvoice.jsx (frontend) and PUT /api/invoices/:id (backend).

2. NEW SYSTEM: `bills` + `payments` + `discharge_payments` tables — 
   updated by the FIFO bill-payment engine when admin uses the 
   "💰 Régler Facture" modal (three payment modes: "libre" = free 
   amount, "specifique" = per-discharge, "integral" = full payment). 
   This hits POST /api/bills/:billId/payments in server.js and writes 
   to `payments` and `discharge_payments` — but it NEVER updates the 
   `invoices` table.

THE BUG
Because of this disconnect, when admin confirms a client payment via 
any of the three bill-payment options:
- The payment IS recorded in `payments` / `discharge_payments` 
  (confirmed by doBillPayConfirm in PageInvoice.jsx)
- The payment is NOT reflected in `invoices.paidAmount`
- The Debt page (PageInvoice.jsx, view==="debts") reads ONLY from 
  `invoices`, so it still shows the client owing the full original 
  amount even after they've paid, in full or in part.
- There's also no journal/audit trail showing "who confirmed what 
  payment when, via which of the three methods" in one unified place.
